[h1]Describe your map. MapGen AI builds it.[/h1]
Type what you want in plain words, like "a ring of mountains with a lake inside" or "add a small island in the middle of the lake". MapGen AI turns it into map settings, and Map Preview shows the result. Keep chatting to adjust it until the map feels right, then start there.

[b]Built for RimWorld 1.6.[/b] Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077]Harmony[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2800857642]Map Preview[/url].
[b]Use your own AI:[/b] Google Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Grok, GLM, Alibaba, a local server such as Ollama or LM Studio, or a custom endpoint.

[h2]What's new in this update[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Edits keep what you built.[/b] Add a lake, then move it, resize it, or change only its outline. Earlier changes stay, and Undo steps back one request at a time.
[*][b]Map ideas with pictures.[/b] Ask for suggestions and get up to three candidates drawn by Map Preview. Refine one, like "make option 2 more natural", before you pick. Your map does not change until you choose.
[*][b]Preference questions.[/b] Not sure what you want? Answer a few multiple-choice questions first. They run on your PC and make no AI calls.
[*][b]Exact or natural shapes.[/b] Ask for a perfect circle, or for a natural lake with an uneven shore.
[*][b]Fill part of an area.[/b] "Fill 70% of the ring's interior with rich soil." Lava fills are available with Odyssey.
[*][b]Place ruins and ancient dangers[/b] by area, direction, or landmark, such as on an island or at the foot of a mountain. Ancient dangers use the game's own generator.
[*][b]Local roads with bridges.[/b] Roads that cross a river or shallow water get wooden bridges automatically.
[*][b]Natural water.[/b] Natural lakes you add now follow the game's own lake style, with shallow edges and lakeshore ground that suits the biome.
[/list]

[h2]How to start[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Subscribe to MapGen AI, Harmony, and Map Preview.
[*]Open [b]Options > Mod settings > MapGen AI[/b]. Pick a provider, enter your API key, and choose a model.
[*]On the world map, select a tile and click [b]✦ AI Map Gen[/b] next to Map Preview.
[*]Type a request, or press [b]Quick suggestions[/b] or [b]Find my preferences[/b].
[*]When the preview looks right, press [b]Generate with these settings[/b]. The settings apply when you start or settle on that tile.
[/olist]

[h2]Example requests[/h2]
[list]
[*]"A ring of mountains with a lake inside and one opening to the south."
[*]"Add a small island in the middle of the lake."
[*]"Move the island to the north side of the lake." / "Make the river straight."
[*]"Fill 70% of the ring's interior with rich soil."
[*]"Put two small ruins on the island."
[*]"Add a dirt road from the west edge to the east edge."
[*]"Add hot springs." (Odyssey)
[*]"Put the coast on the north side."
[*]"Recommend a map." / "Make option 2 more natural."
[/list]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]It shapes the map you are about to start.[/b] It does not edit a colony map you are already playing.
[*][b]World rivers and coasts stay connected.[/b] You can straighten a river or turn a coast, but you cannot delete them or add a coast inland.
[*][b]Map Preview shows terrain and layout.[/b] Plants, building interiors, and loot appear when the real map is generated.
[*][b]Results depend on the model.[/b] Check the preview, and use Undo or Reset when a request goes wrong.
[*][b]DLC and landmark mods:[/b] you can add hot springs, fjords, oasis, and other tile features when the DLC or mod is active and the tile allows them.
[*][b]Cost and privacy:[/b] each message is a request to the provider you chose, paid with your own key. A few actions add a request, such as summarizing a long chat or re-asking when a reply comes back malformed. Your key is saved in your RimWorld mod settings on your PC.
[*][b]Languages:[/b] menus in English, Korean, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese. Some newer messages appear in English for Japanese and Chinese.
[*][b]Image input is paused[/b] in this version.
[*][b]Presets:[/b] Save and Load keep map settings you want to reuse.
[/list]
