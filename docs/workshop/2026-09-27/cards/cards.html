DESCRIBE IT

Select a tile on the world map and open ✦ AI Map Gen. The chat opens next to Map Preview.

Type what you want. The reply lists exactly what changed.

1 · TYPE

Describe the map in plain words, or press Quick suggestions.

2 · PREVIEW

The AI turns your words into map settings. Map Preview redraws the tile.

3 · GENERATE

Press Generate with these settings, then start or settle there.
